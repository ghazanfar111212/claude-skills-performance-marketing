# Scoring Methodology

How to convert research observations into tiers, grids, and the 0 - 100 Opportunity Score. The purpose of this rubric is consistency: two different runs on the same niche should land on roughly the same scores, and every score should be defensible from evidence.

## 1. Demand tiers

Assign from observed evidence, not intuition:

| Tier | Evidence looks like |
|---|---|
| **Very High** | Constant fresh discussion (multiple new Reddit/forum threads weekly), mainstream news coverage, 500k+ view YouTube videos in the past year, autocomplete full of variations |
| **High** | Regular discussion (weekly-ish threads), several 100k+ view videos, rich question landscape ("vs", "best", "how to" all populated) |
| **Moderate** | Steady but slower discussion (monthly threads), 10k - 100k view videos, decent question variety |
| **Low** | Sparse discussion, old threads dominate, few videos, thin autocomplete |
| **Niche** | Tiny but identifiable audience; discussion concentrated in one or two specialist communities. Low volume ≠ low value - niche + commercial intent can be excellent |

## 2. Supply / competition tiers

| Tier | Evidence looks like |
|---|---|
| **Saturated** | SERP full of fresh (≤12 months), deep content from strong brands; multiple dedicated sites; heavy AI-content flood on top of it |
| **Strong** | Good content from strong domains, but with visible staleness or format gaps |
| **Moderate** | Mixed SERP - some good content, some thin/dated; niche sites can rank |
| **Weak** | SERP dominated by forum threads, outdated posts (2+ years), thin listicles, or generic AI content |
| **Open** | Searches return barely-relevant results; questions asked repeatedly on Reddit with no good canonical answer anywhere |

Key nuance: **Reddit threads ranking on page 1 is the single strongest "weak supply" signal** - Google is ranking raw discussion because no one has written the definitive piece.

## 3. The Demand × Supply grid

Place every candidate topic in the grid. The opportunity zone is the top-right:

```
                 Supply: Open/Weak      Moderate           Strong/Saturated
Demand: V.High   ★ PRIORITY 1          Strong play        Only with unique angle
High             ★ PRIORITY 1          Good play          Usually skip
Moderate         Priority 2            Selective          Skip
Low/Niche        Only if commercial    Skip               Skip
                 intent is high
```

## 4. Opportunity Score (0 - 100)

Weighted composite. Score each component 0 - 100 using the anchors, then combine:

| Component | Weight | Anchors |
|---|---|---|
| **Demand** | 25% | Very High=90 - 100, High=70 - 85, Moderate=45 - 65, Low=20 - 40, Niche=15 - 45 (raise within band if audience is valuable) |
| **Competition (inverted)** | 25% | Open=90 - 100, Weak=70 - 85, Moderate=45 - 65, Strong=20 - 40, Saturated=5 - 20 |
| **Commercial/monetization potential** | 20% | Clear purchase intent + multiple monetization paths=85 - 100; indirect (lead gen, audience building)=50 - 75; purely informational with no path=10 - 40 |
| **Momentum** | 15% | Clearly rising=85 - 100; stable evergreen=60 - 75; seasonal=score at 55 - 70 and flag timing; declining=10 - 35 |
| **Execution fit** | 15% | Achievable with content alone=80 - 100; needs tools/calculators=60 - 80 (harder but defensible); needs proprietary data/authority the user lacks=20 - 50 |

**Adjustments after the weighted sum:**
- AI-saturation penalty: −5 to −15 if the SERP is flooded with AI content AND the user has no differentiation lever (experience, data, tools, brand).
- Defensibility bonus: +5 to +10 if the recommended asset is hard to copy (calculator, original research, template pack).
- Cap at 95. A 96+ score implies certainty that research sampling can't provide.

**Presentation format - always show the breakdown:**

```
Opportunity Score: 84/100
  Demand: High (78) - weekly Reddit threads, 3 videos >200k views this year
  Competition: Weak (80) - page 1 is Reddit threads + 2022 posts
  Commercial: High (85) - direct affiliate products, $100 - 400 price range
  Momentum: Rising (75) - discussion volume visibly up since late 2025
  Execution: Good fit (70) - winnable with a definitive guide + comparison table
  Recommendation: Create within 2 weeks; momentum window is open.
```

## 5. Interpretation bands

- **85 - 95: Act now.** Rare alignment - put it at the top of the calendar.
- **70 - 84: Strong.** Core of the 30-day plan.
- **55 - 69: Selective.** Worth it if it clusters with stronger topics or serves a strategic goal (topical authority, lead magnet).
- **40 - 54: Backlog.** Revisit if conditions change.
- **<40: Skip** and say why - a documented "no" is valuable output too.

## 6. Monetization ranking

For each priority opportunity, rank the viable paths. Rate each path Strong / Possible / Weak with one line of reasoning:

affiliate, display ads, lead generation, digital product (course/template), SaaS/tool, newsletter, community, consulting/services, ecommerce.

Match to the user's stated model: an 84-score topic with Weak fit for the user's actual business model should be flagged as such ("great topic, wrong monetization shape for you - unless you want to expand into X").

## 7. Virality / platform fit scoring

Rate each relevant platform Low / Medium / High with mechanism-based reasoning, e.g.:
- Google: wins on question-shaped, evergreen demand with weak supply
- YouTube: wins on visual/demonstrable topics and "watch before buying" decisions
- TikTok/Reels: wins on surprise, transformation, strong hooks, sub-60s payoff
- LinkedIn: wins on career/business relevance + contrarian or data-led POV
- Reddit: wins on genuine utility and native tone; dies on anything promotional
- Pinterest: wins on planning-mode verticals (home, food, weddings, fashion, DIY)

Never claim a piece "will go viral." Claim conditional likelihood: "High TikTok fit *if* the hook shows the before/after in the first 2 seconds."

## 8. AI Saturation Score

Rate the niche Low / Medium / High saturation based on SERP sampling (telltales: interchangeable listicles, no first-hand detail, identical structures, no named authors). Then answer the only question that matters: **what differentiation lever still wins here?** Options, in rough order of power: original data/research → interactive tools → genuine first-hand experience → strong contrarian POV → superior format (video where text dominates, etc.). If no lever is available to this user, downgrade the opportunity regardless of demand.
