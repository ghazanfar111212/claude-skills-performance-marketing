# Full Report Template

Use this structure for Full mode only. Produce as a Markdown file. Every section must earn its length - a section with nothing substantive gets 2 - 3 sentences and moves on, not padding. Target 2,500 - 5,000 words for the whole report depending on niche complexity.

Throughout: numbers are tiered estimates unless sourced (state the basis inline); tables for grids/scores/calendars, prose for reasoning.

---

# Content Market Intelligence Report: [Niche]

*Prepared [date] · Research basis: [N] web searches across [sources sampled] · All demand/competition figures are directional estimates from live sampling unless cited*

## 1. Executive Summary
The 60-second version: the market in 2 - 3 sentences, the top 3 opportunities with scores, the single recommended first move, and the biggest risk. A reader who stops here should still know what to do.

## 2. Market Overview
Size and shape of the space: who the audience segments are, what's driving interest now, the sub-niches identified (if the market was broad), and which sub-niche this report goes deep on and why.

## 3. Trending Topics
Table: topic · momentum (rising/stable/seasonal/declining) · evidence · estimated lifespan · seasonal vs evergreen. 5 - 10 rows. Follow with 1 - 2 paragraphs on the meta-trend.

## 4. Demand vs Supply Analysis
The 2×2 grid (from scoring-methodology.md) with all candidate topics placed. Then narrate the opportunity zone: for each high-demand/low-supply topic, the specific evidence on both sides.

## 5. Search Intent
For each priority topic: the intent mix and the top 5 - 10 real questions people ask (verbatim where captured). Group into informational / commercial / transactional / comparison.

## 6. Audience Pain Points
Ranked list, in the audience's own words where possible. For each: how often it repeats, where it shows up, and what content shape would resolve it.

## 7. Keyword Intelligence
Clustered, not listed. Each cluster = one content asset: cluster name · primary keyword · supporting/long-tail/question keywords · intent · competition tier. Flag emerging and low-competition clusters explicitly.

## 8. Competitor Analysis
Top 3 - 6 players: who they are, what they do well, specific weaknesses observed (stale dates, thin sections, no tools, poor mobile UX, missing formats), and the outflanking angle per competitor.

## 9. Content Gaps
The gaps you verified by searching and finding nothing good: unanswered questions, missing comparisons, missing tutorials/calculators/templates/case studies. Each gap: what's missing · proof it's missing · what to build.

## 10. Opportunity Scores
Full scored table of all evaluated opportunities (score · demand · competition · commercial · momentum · execution), then the detailed breakdown block (per scoring-methodology.md format) for the top 3 - 5.

## 11. Monetization Potential
Per top opportunity: ranked monetization paths with Strong/Possible/Weak ratings and reasoning, matched against the user's business model.

## 12. Recommended Content Ideas
10 - 20 concrete ideas across formats (blog, video, tool, calculator, template, lead magnet, thread, etc.). Each: title-level specificity · format · target cluster · one-line "why it will perform" (demand evidence + supply weakness). Ideas without a why don't ship.

## 13. 30/60/90-Day Content Plan
Three tables (or one with a phase column): asset · format · target keyword cluster · opportunity score · funnel stage · rationale for timing. Days 1 - 30 = quick wins + momentum topics; 31 - 60 = pillar assets; 61 - 90 = tools/defensible assets + seasonal positioning.

## 14. Growth Forecast
3/6/12-month directional outlook (growing/stable/declining) with reasoning, key assumptions, and what would change the forecast. Qualitative unless dated evidence supports specifics.

## 15. Immediate Action Plan
Close with decisive lists:
- **Top 10 opportunities** (ranked, scores)
- **Quick wins** (publishable within 2 weeks)
- **Long-term plays** (defensible assets worth 60 - 90 day investment)
- **Hidden gems** (non-obvious finds - underserved audiences, missing formats)
- **High-value keyword clusters** to verify in a keyword tool before scaling spend
- **Create this week:** the single first asset, with the why
- **Potential lead magnets / products / SaaS ideas** surfaced by the research (if any genuinely emerged - don't force it)
