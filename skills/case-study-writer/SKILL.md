# Case Study Writer

## When to Use This Skill
- Writing a case study from raw client data
- Restructuring an existing case study for better conversion
- Building a case study library for your agency
- Adapting one case study into multiple formats (PDF, landing page, LinkedIn post, cold email snippet)
- Preparing case studies for a specific vertical or offer

## The PAR Framework (Core Structure)

Every case study follows this 3-part structure:

### P - Problem (The Before State)
- Client's business context (size, vertical, location)
- Specific situation before they hired you
- Metrics showing the pain (low leads, high CPA, declining revenue)
- What they tried that didn't work
- What was at stake if they didn't fix it

### A - Action (What You Did)
- Strategy decisions (and why)
- Specific tactics executed
- Timeline of implementation
- Tools/platforms used
- Key insight or differentiator (what others would have missed)

### R - Result (The After State)
- Headline metric (the big number)
- Supporting metrics (secondary wins)
- Timeline for results
- Business impact (not just marketing metrics)
- Direct client quote if possible

## Case Study Formats

### Format 1: Long-form (Website / PDF)
- 800-1,500 words
- Full PAR structure with detailed narrative
- Multiple data visualizations
- Client quote block
- CTA to work with you

### Format 2: One-Pager (Sales Deck)
- 150-300 words
- Stripped to headline metric + 3 supporting points
- Logo + industry + timeframe at top
- Single hero number dominant

### Format 3: LinkedIn Post / Social
- 200-400 words
- Hook → Problem → Action → Result → Takeaway
- Line breaks for scannability
- Specific numbers in the hook

### Format 4: Email/Outreach Snippet
- 50-80 words
- Problem-Result compression, Action implied
- One number, one specific detail

## Writing Frameworks By Vertical

### Paid Ads Case Study (Google Ads / Meta)
**Problem anchors to cover:**
- Current CPA vs. target CPA
- Wasted spend (broad match, no negatives, bad structure)
- Conversion tracking gaps
- Audience or creative issues

**Action anchors to cover:**
- Account restructure decision
- Campaign architecture chosen
- Creative/ad copy approach
- Tracking fixes
- Bidding strategy shift

**Result anchors to cover:**
- CPA change (%)
- Conversion volume change
- ROAS or CPL
- Monthly revenue impact

### Example headline metrics (paid ads):
- "Reduced cost-per-lead from $247 to $89 in 60 days"
- "3.2x increase in qualified consultations for dental practice"
- "Cut wasted ad spend by $8,400/month while doubling leads"

### Lead Gen / Funnel Case Study
**Problem anchors:**
- Low conversion rate on landing page/funnel
- Long sales cycle
- Poor lead quality

**Action anchors:**
- Funnel redesign approach
- Lead qualification added
- Automation sequences built
- Tech stack changes

**Result anchors:**
- Conversion rate improvement
- Lead-to-appointment rate
- Time-to-close reduction
- Revenue per lead

### Agency/Consulting Case Study
**Problem anchors:**
- Specific business goal not hit
- Operational bottleneck
- Strategic gap

**Action anchors:**
- Systems/process built
- Team enablement
- Measurement framework

**Result anchors:**
- Business outcome (revenue, margin, retention)
- Efficiency gain
- Team/client NPS

### Ecommerce Case Study
**Problem anchors:**
- ROAS below breakeven
- High CAC vs. AOV
- Creative fatigue / scaling wall

**Action anchors:**
- Account restructure (ASC, creative systems)
- Creative testing framework
- Email/SMS lifecycle built
- Attribution fixes

**Result anchors:**
- ROAS
- Revenue growth
- LTV/CAC ratio

## Metric Presentation Rules

### The Headline Number
Your case study lives or dies on one number. Make it:
- **Specific**: "247%" not "more than 2x"
- **Contextual**: "in 90 days" or "with the same budget"
- **Relevant**: The metric the reader actually cares about

### Supporting Metrics (3-5 max)
Group by category:
- **Traffic/Reach**: Impressions, clicks, CTR
- **Conversion**: CPA, CVR, leads/sales
- **Business**: Revenue, ROAS, LTV

### What to Avoid
- Vanity metrics without business context (impressions alone)
- Percentages without absolute numbers ("500% increase!" from 1 to 5)
- Graphs without axis labels
- Comparing apples to oranges (different time ranges, attribution windows)

### When Client Won't Let You Share Numbers
Use directional language:
- "Doubled qualified leads within 90 days"
- "Reduced CPA by more than half"
- "Scaled to 6-figure monthly ad spend profitably"

Or anonymize: "A $5M revenue home services company"

## Client Quote Formula

Best quotes cover one of:
- **Outcome**: "We had to hire 2 more technicians to keep up"
- **Process**: "They showed up, did the work, and delivered"
- **Transformation**: "Before working with this team, I was ready to shut down ads entirely"

### Getting Good Quotes
- Ask open-ended questions: "What was the hardest part before we started?"
- Record the call, transcribe, extract best 1-3 sentences
- Edit lightly for readability; keep voice authentic
- Get written approval before publishing

## Visual Elements

### Must-Have Visuals
- Client logo (get permission, use correctly)
- Hero metric as large number graphic
- Before/after screenshots (dashboards, landing pages, ads)
- Timeline graphic showing key milestones
- Headshot + name + title of quoted person

### Dashboard Screenshots
- Blur out account IDs, budgets if sensitive
- Crop to the relevant metric, not the whole dashboard
- Annotate with arrows/callouts for clarity
- Compare date ranges side-by-side when possible

## Repurposing One Case Study

From a single client win, create:
1. **Long-form case study** (website + PDF)
2. **One-pager** (sales deck)
3. **LinkedIn carousel post** (7-10 slides)
4. **LinkedIn text post**
5. **Cold email snippet** (1-2 sentence version)
6. **Sales call talk track** (memorized 60-second version)
7. **Video testimonial** (if client agrees)
8. **Before/after Instagram post** (if visual-friendly)
9. **Podcast talking point** (if you do media)

## Case Study Template (Ready to Fill)

```
CLIENT: [Name or "A $X revenue [vertical] company"]
INDUSTRY: [Specific vertical]
LOCATION: [City, Country]
ENGAGEMENT: [Month/Year - Month/Year, or "Ongoing since X"]

HEADLINE:
[Outcome-focused, specific, with timeframe]

THE PROBLEM:
[Client's situation in their words/metrics]
[What they'd tried that failed]
[What was at stake]

THE APPROACH:
[Strategic decision 1 + why]
[Strategic decision 2 + why]
[Strategic decision 3 + why]
[Tools/systems deployed]
[Timeline of execution]

THE RESULTS:
• Headline metric: [X% improvement / $Y generated]
• Supporting metric 1
• Supporting metric 2
• Supporting metric 3
• Business outcome

CLIENT QUOTE:
"[2-3 sentences in client's voice]" - [Name], [Title], [Company]

NEXT STEPS:
[Current engagement status, what's next]
```

## Anti-Patterns
- Writing "we did X, Y, Z" without explaining *why* each decision was made
- Leading with your process instead of client's problem
- Burying the result at the bottom
- Using jargon the prospect doesn't understand
- Claims without numbers or concrete evidence
- Generic "we delivered amazing results" language
- No timeframe (was that "2x growth" over 10 years or 90 days?)
- Stolen stock photo headshots - readers spot these immediately
- Making the agency the hero instead of the client

## Ethics & Approval
- Always get written approval before publishing
- Respect NDAs - some clients can only be anonymized
- Don't inflate or manipulate numbers
- Disclose material limitations (seasonal effects, other variables)
- If client asks to remove later, honor it
