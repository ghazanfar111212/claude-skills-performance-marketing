# Formats, Length, Pillars & Series

## Choosing a format

| Format | Best for | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Long-form tutorial / how-to | Search, evergreen, authority | 6 - 15 min | Match title to search phrasing; deliver fast, no padding |
| Listicle / ranking / tier list | Browse + search, easy to package | 8 - 14 min | Each item is a mini-hook; strongest item teased in intro |
| Story / documentary | Browse, high retention, loyalty | 10 - 20+ min | Needs a real narrative arc; retention lives on curiosity loops |
| "I tried X for N days" | Browse, transformation, relatable | 8 - 15 min | Stakes + result; thumbnail shows the payoff |
| Versus / comparison | Search, high commercial intent | 8 - 14 min | Clear verdict; good for buyer-intent niches |
| Reaction / teardown | Browse, fast to produce | 8 - 20 min | React with insight, not just watching; add value |
| Short (<60s) | Reach, top-of-funnel, testing ideas | 15 - 60s | Hook in first 1 - 2s; loopable; one idea only |

**Shorts vs long-form strategy:** Shorts win reach and discovery but convert poorly to watch time and subscribers; long-form builds the actual channel and revenue. Use Shorts to *test hooks and topics cheaply* and to funnel, not as the whole strategy. A strong long-form concept can spawn 3 - 5 Shorts; a Short that overperforms is a signal to build the long-form version.

## Content pillars

A pillar is a recurring theme the channel owns. 3 - 5 pillars keep a channel legible to the algorithm (clear topical identity) and to viewers (they know what they'll get). Every idea should map to a pillar; if it maps to none, it's either a new pillar or a distraction.

Example (a home-espresso channel):
- Pillar 1: Beginner setup & buying guides (search/evergreen)
- Pillar 2: Technique & dialing-in (search + loyalty)
- Pillar 3: Gear reviews & versus (commercial intent)
- Pillar 4: "I tried…" experiments & taste tests (browse/entertainment)

Balance pillars across the two engines so the channel has both compounding evergreen floors and high-ceiling browse swings.

## Series & binge arcs

Series pull binge sessions, which the algorithm rewards with more suggested-video placement.

- **Sequential series**: Part 1→N, or "Day 1 / Day 10 / Day 30." End each with an open loop into the next.
- **Themed runs**: same format applied across items ("I ranked every X," one per week).
- **Playlists by pillar**: group evergreen videos so one view pulls the next.

When building a full package, chain 2 - 4 ideas into at least one arc, and note the connective hook between them.

## Priority / shooting order

Sequence the ideas to film by weighing:
1. **Packageability** - can it be made irresistible? Highest weight.
2. **Demand strength** - how much validated interest you observed.
3. **Effort to produce** - favor high-ceiling, low-effort videos early for quick wins.
4. **Momentum fit** - does it build on what the channel's current audience already responds to?

Open with 1 - 2 "get a win" videos (high ceiling, low effort, strong packaging), then alternate evergreen (floor-builders) with browse swings (ceiling-chasers).
