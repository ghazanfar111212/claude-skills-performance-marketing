# Account Safety, Scheduling & Benchmarks

A restricted LinkedIn account costs far more than a slightly slower ramp. On a sender that's a real professional asset, always err conservative.

## Daily limits

- **Connection requests:** ~15 - 25/day on a warmed account. LinkedIn throttles around **~100 - 200 invites/week** and tightens fast when accept rate is low. On a **fresh or reconnected account, start at ~15 - 20/day.**
- **Messages** (to existing connections): ~40/day is low-risk - messaging 1st-degree connections is fine.
- **InMails** (to non-connections, via Sales Nav/Premium): the real limiter is your monthly InMail credit allotment (~50/mo on Sales Nav), not the tool's cap. Use deliberately; don't make InMail a default sequence step.
- **Profile views:** generous, but automated views still count toward looking-like-a-bot; keep to one per lead in-sequence.

## The fresh-account ramp (first 2 weeks)

A just-connected/reconnected account should not launch at full volume.

- **Days 1 - 3:** warm manually - log in, engage, comment, post once. No automated volume.
- **Week 1:** ~15 connection requests/day.
- **Week 2:** ~20/day if accept rate is healthy (>30%).
- **Week 3+:** add ~5/week only while accept rate holds. Back off immediately if it drops or LinkedIn shows any warning.
- Withdraw stale pending invites monthly - a pile of ignored invites drags your accept rate and flags you.

## Scheduling (by prospect timezone, not yours)

Set the campaign to the **prospect's** business hours - a DM landing at 3am their time reads as automation and gets buried.

- **US list:** schedule roughly **8:00 AM - 2:00 PM Eastern**, weekdays. That catches the high-engagement morning window across ET/CT and reaches into PT morning.
- For an operator in a distant timezone (e.g. Pakistan), the US-morning window also falls in *their* evening (~6 - 11pm PKT) - a normal, native-looking activity window for the account, and it keeps the operator awake to reply to live leads.
- **Weekdays only.** B2B engagement craters on weekends. Tue - Thu is the peak if tightening further.
- Never run 24/7 - continuous activity is the single most obvious automation flag. Let the tool randomize sends within the window.

## Cloud vs. browser-extension tools

- Prefer **cloud-based** automation (HeyReach, Expandi, Dripify): runs on their servers 24/7, uses a location-matched proxy, doesn't depend on your browser session, and enforces safe caps.
- Avoid **browser extensions** (e.g. Waalaxy in-browser) for volume: tied to your live session, riskier, and harder to control limits.
- **Don't run two automation tools on one LinkedIn account** - even a dead session from an old tool plus a live one invites flags. Fully disconnect the old one first.

## Benchmarks (rough, value-first LinkedIn)

- Connection accept rate: **30 - 40%+** with a strong profile + relevant (or no) note.
- Accept → reply: **15 - 25%** with a specific finding; ~8 - 12% with a category insight; ~2 - 5% generic.
- Reply → payoff delivered: 40 - 60%.
- Payoff → booked call: 20 - 35%.
- The **finding-first (or at least insight-first) opener is the single biggest lever** on reply rate. A generic offer opener sits near 2 - 5% no matter how polished.

If accept rate is under ~25% → targeting or profile is off (fix before scaling). If reply rate is low but accepts are fine → the hook/copy is the problem, not the list.

## Compliance & etiquette

- **LinkedIn ToS:** manual outreach is fine; automation/scraping is against ToS - use conservatively, at your own account risk, never at full speed on a fresh account.
- **Respect opt-outs immediately.** "Remove me" / "not interested" = stop, permanently. One ignored opt-out can generate a report that restricts the account.
- **Don't scrape/store prospect data at scale** from a fresh account - build the list properly via Sales Nav.
